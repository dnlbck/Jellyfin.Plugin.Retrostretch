Retro Stretch

Pre-stretches 4:3 sources to fill a 16:9 frame at transcode time, so an HDMI-to-RCA converter feeding a CRT TV renders correct 4:3 geometry instead of pillared / squished output. Only kicks in when the requesting device matches one of the identifiers below AND Jellyfin is actively re-encoding video (not direct-play / video-copy).

Master toggle. Turning off leaves Jellyfin's transcodes untouched.
